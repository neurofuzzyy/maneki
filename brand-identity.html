1_tendril tattoo

Tendril is a conceptual tattoo studio that specializes in custom sigils — symbolic designs created in collaboration with each client. Each sigil encodes personal mantras, memories, or affirmations, resulting in tattoos that are intimate and deeply individual.

Visually, the designs draw from organic patterns found in nature, evoking resilience, memory, and transformation. Tendril offers an alternative to traditional tattooing by turning inward meaning into outward mark.
