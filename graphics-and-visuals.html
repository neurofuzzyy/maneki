1_1 in 292 million—an exploration of lotteries in America

1 in 292 Million is an in-depth exploration of lottery systems in America, examining the structure and impact of the Powerball lottery. Through data analysis and visualization, the broadsheet investigates the financial and social effects of the lottery, highlighting its role in society and its influence on different communities.
